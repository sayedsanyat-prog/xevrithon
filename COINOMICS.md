XEVRITHON — Coinomics

1. Purpose

This document describes the planned economic structure of the XEVRITHON cryptocurrency.

2. Maximum Supply

The maximum supply of XEVRITHON is:

17,000,000 XEVRITHON coins

The protocol must enforce this limit through independently verifiable consensus rules.

3. Coin Creation

New coins are planned to be created through the blockchain's block reward mechanism.

The exact reward mechanism has not yet been selected.

4. Mining Rewards

The following parameters remain undecided:

- Initial block reward
- Reward reduction schedule
- Halving or alternative reward mechanism
- Block interval
- Final coin issuance rules

No reward amount is final until documented and reviewed.

5. Transaction Fees

The protocol will define transaction fees.

The following details require further design:

- Minimum relay fee
- Fee calculation method
- Miner fee collection
- Fee handling when the maximum supply is reached

6. Supply Validation

Every full node must independently verify that:

- Block rewards follow the protocol.
- No block creates unauthorized coins.
- The maximum supply is never exceeded.
- Transaction inputs and outputs are valid.
- The same transaction output cannot be spent twice.

7. Coin Distribution

The initial distribution model has not been finalized.

Any premine, founder allocation, community allocation, or other distribution must be explicitly documented before implementation.

8. Economic Transparency

All issuance rules must be public, deterministic, and independently verifiable.

The project must not promise a guaranteed market price or investment return.

9. Current Status

The maximum supply is selected.

The complete issuance and reward schedule remains under design.
